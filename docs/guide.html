Requirements

  • macOS 11 (Big Sur) or newer. Works on Apple Silicon (M1–M4) and Intel Macs.
  • Ableton Live 10.1 or newer (or any DAW that loads VST3 or Audio Units). Sidechain needs Live 11 or newer.

1 Download and unzip

  • From email: click the attachment Gate Daddy.zip → Download.
  • From Google Drive: right-click the file → Download.

Open your Downloads folder and double-click the .zip. A folder appears containing the installer and this guide.

2 Quit Ableton Live

Save and quit Live completely (⌘Q). Plugins can't be installed while Live has them loaded.

3 Open the installer (macOS security step)

Double-click Install Gate Daddy 1.0.0.pkg. Because this is an independent plugin (not from the App Store), macOS will most likely stop it the first time:

“Apple could not verify ‘Install Gate Daddy 1.0.0.pkg’ is free of malware…” Normal for independent audio plugins. Click Done. Do not click “Move to Trash”.

macOS 15 Sequoia and newer (and most macOS 13–14 setups)

  1. Open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to Security. You'll see “Install Gate Daddy 1.0.0.pkg was blocked…”
  3. Click Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway again.

macOS 14 Sonoma and older (shortcut)

Control-click (or right-click) the .pkg → Open → Open.

4 Run the installer

  1. Click Continue → Install, and enter your Mac password.
  2. When it says The installation was successful, click Close.

5 Turn on plugins in Ableton and rescan

  1. Open Live → Live menu → Settings (Preferences in Live 11 and older) → Plug-Ins tab.
  2. Turn ON Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders and Use Audio Units v2 (“Use Audio Units” in Live 11).
  3. Hold Option (⌥) and click Rescan.

6 Load it

  1. In Live's browser: Plug-Ins → Gate Daddy Labs → drag Gate Daddy onto a bass, pad, synth or vocal track. VST3 and AU both work; pick one.
  2. Click the wrench/window icon on the device to open Dr. Gate's clinic.
Gate Daddy: presets and REALITY CHECK up top, the volume shaper and trance gate in the middle, Mood Swing LFOs and sidechain on the right.

7 Set up the sidechain (kick ducking)

  1. On the Gate Daddy device in Ableton, click the small triangle ▸ in the device's title bar to show the Sidechain section.
  2. Switch Sidechain on, and in Audio From choose your kick track.
  3. In Gate Daddy's SIDECHAIN panel, click ON and set source to EXTERNAL. It should say “SIDECHAIN CONNECTED. SOMEONE CARES.”
  4. Set THRESH so the red level bar crosses the white line on each kick, then dial in AMOUNT and RELEASE (longer = more pump).
Pro move: kick-triggered pumpIn the VOLUME SHAPER, set the mode to SC TRIG. Your drawn curve now restarts on every kick, even off-grid ones. Turn on HOLD END to play it once per hit.

8 Quick tour

MODULEWHAT IT DOES
TRANCE GATE16-step gate. Pick the rate (1/4 down to 1/64, including triplets and dotted). Click steps on/off, Alt-drag to set step volume. Buttons: 4 FLOOR, TRANCE, OFFBEAT, GALLOP, CHOP, ALL ON, CHAOS. Knobs: steps, length, swing, attack, release, mix.
VOLUME SHAPERDraw a volume curve. Click to add a point, drag to move it, double-click to delete it, and drag the pink diamonds to bend the curve. Right-click for invert/reverse/double. Modes: SYNC, FREE HZ, INPUT TRIG, SC TRIG. BAND lets you shape only the lows or only the highs.
TRANSIENTSPunch up or soften the front of each hit (Attack) and the tail (Sustain). Runs first in the chain.
LOW-END DUCKDucks only the bass below FREQ, following the curve or the sidechain. Fixes kick/bass fights.
MOOD SWING 1 & 2LFOs that modulate cutoff, resonance, pan, volume, drive or width. Sync to tempo or run free.
FILTER / THE VERDICTLow/high/band-pass filter, drive, stereo width, dry/wet mix, output, and the PAIN meter (how hard it's turning you down).
REALITY CHECKRandomizes the curve, the gate pattern and the rates. Press until it slaps.
DENIAL MODEBypass. For before/after comparisons (or denial).

Hover over any knob for a tooltip. Click Dr. Gate for a fresh diagnosis.

Presets to try first

  • GET REAL PUMP: classic 1/4 sidechain-style pump, no routing needed.
  • HERE'S THE DEAL (1/16 GATE): trance gate on pads and chords.
  • LOWS-ONLY ACCOUNTABILITY: pumps only the bass, leaves the highs alone.
  • THE MOUSTACHE WOBBLE: LFO filter wobble for basses.
  • SIDECHAIN INTERVENTION: real kick ducking (needs step 7).

9 Updates

Gate Daddy checks for new versions when you open it. When one is out, an orange UPDATE vX.X.X button appears next to the logo and Dr. Gate lets you know. Click it, run the new installer the same way, and restart Ableton.

It doesn't show up in AbletonCheck step 5: both switches on, then hold Option and click Rescan. Restart Live if needed.
